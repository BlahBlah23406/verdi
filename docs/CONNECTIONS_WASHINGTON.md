# Connections: Redmond → greater Seattle → Washington

Research checked **2026-11-19 UTC** (November 18 Pacific). These are **documented capabilities and proposed adapters**, not connected Verdi accounts. No real customer meter, ORCA export, bank account, vehicle or receipt has been tested in this work. Current app ingestion remains foreground phone trip summaries and normalized daily electricity CSV; provider-native parsers and account authorization are separate implementation tasks.

Usefulness means the input can improve measured activity, resolve an accounting gap, or help explain an estimate. An accessible API is not necessarily personal activity evidence. A provider's coverage claim is not proof that a particular account is supported.

## 1. Redmond first

Select providers from the **service address, utility bill and service dates**, not postal city or ZIP alone. Ask which utilities are included in rent, household size/sharing, vehicles, dietary preferences, and whether work or another person paid for travel. Record periods with no access as missing coverage.

P0 = first personal pilot; P1 = next after sample/authorization and factor validation; P2 = later or contextual until missing units/factors are resolved. Priority is a product decision, not a claim that every resident has the activity.

For automatic utility sync, evaluate an authorized intermediary such as Arcadia separately from customer downloads. Its [current provider-coverage guide](https://docs.arcadia.com/docs/provider-coverage) distinguishes statement support, interval support, service types, history, MFA and provider-specific requirements; its [quick start](https://docs.arcadia.com/docs/api-quick-start-guide) notes that interval activation is opt-in by meter. This verifies the integration route, **not PSE residential coverage**. Before choosing it, inspect the current catalog for the exact provider/account type, confirm electric and gas separately, obtain commercial access, and reconcile one owner-authorized meter. Keep status “coverage unverified” until that succeeds. Do not collect utility passwords in Verdi or promise uninterrupted sync; use the provider's authorized connection flow and report stale/revoked access.

## Connect the whole life without manufacturing completeness

The intended inventory includes home electricity and heating; personal and shared travel, flights and lodging; food and dining; clothing/electronics/furniture and deliveries; household services; water/wastewater; landfill/compost/recycling; and, explicitly separately, embodied housing/vehicle impacts. A shared household, campus meal plan, cash purchase, employer-paid flight, included-in-rent energy, repaired/secondhand good or gifted item may be invisible to banking. Offer narrowly scoped manual/statement inputs and mark them estimated or unmeasured.

Building area, household membership and occupancy dates are allocation context supplied by the user. Rent or mortgage payments are not direct measurements of building embodied carbon. Public parcel/building records, charging-station maps, weather and city inventories may enrich explanations but do not prove personal activity. Do not connect health, calendar, work, or full mailbox data merely to increase connector count; only request a source when it closes a defined gap with a clear user benefit.

## Acceptance gates before calling a connection supported

1. Obtain a consented, de-identified sample for that provider/account type and document retention/revocation. No credential sharing or portal scraping as a default integration.
2. Preserve provider/account/meter IDs, original units, local timezone and UTC interval, revisions, actual/estimated flags and import/export direction. Normalize DST and zero-consumption records deliberately.
3. Reconcile totals against the same bill/service interval and explain discrepancies, rather than compare calendar totals with billing totals. Test repeat upload, corrections, overlapping windows, multiple meters and a second household.
4. Select a sourced, versioned factor with matching denominator, geography, year and accounting boundary. Missing factor means unresolved evidence, not zero emissions.
5. Verify cross-source matching: physical use replaces the matching spend component; receipt replaces only matching line items; no topup/repayment emissions; home EV charging remains inside household electricity once.
6. Show status separately: research verified → sample tested → adapter tested → authorized → synced through date → stale/revoked. Only successful real-account tests justify the final states. Coverage includes category and date range, not a connector count or invented completeness percentage.

First deliverable should be a reconciled PSE electricity + gas month, confirmed phone/ORCA travel, and a receipt/bank coverage review for one Redmond household. Expand to Seattle City Light and SnoPUD after that reference case passes; expand statewide provider by provider. This yields a useful partial inventory with an honest gap list before attempting a full consumption footprint.
