FROM EVERYDAY DATA TO AN ESTIMATE

A footprint with a paper trail.

01 / COLLECT

Start with evidence.

Import daily electricity readings from a CSV, or log a journey manually. Foreground phone trip detection is implemented for Android and iOS.

02 / REVIEW

Keep people in control.

Review imported candidates and check overlaps before confirming. Only confirmed activities enter the footprint.

03 / UNDERSTAND

See what adds up.

Explore monthly totals, inspect each activity's factor and source, and compare travel alternatives in the Reduce tab.

THE MULTI-SOURCE VISION

More of life, connected.

Live utility connections, purchase and flight integrations, waste tracking, background sensing, and verified rewards are roadmap work. Current purchase, flight, and waste interfaces are scaffolding; their factors are placeholders.

US pilot estimates cover logged activities, not a complete lifecycle footprint. Missing data does not mean zero emissions. Rewards are preview-only and cannot be redeemed. Device and pilot validation remain ahead.
